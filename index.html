From the Airbnb to the lifts

Walk to Super-Châtel

About 650 m to the gondola in the village center, roughly 10 minutes in ski boots. Used on Friday and Sunday.

Walking directions

Free ski shuttles

Line 3 (Centre / Béchigne / Jardy / Linga) stops at Béchigne and runs to the Linga lifts. Line 1 runs from the center to Linga and Pré-la-Joux. Both ran about every 15 minutes last season, from 8:30 and 8:00 AM. The Pysae app shows live times.
